# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Prospective clients and collaborators (studios, founders, teams) evaluating Dahiana Porto as a low-code and AI builder for products, automations, and SEO/AEO work. They arrive from a shared link or search, spend a couple of minutes deciding whether she's credible and worth a message, and act on that impression immediately (email CTA) or leave.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio site that establishes credibility fast and converts interest into an email inquiry. Success is a visitor understanding what Dahiana does and who she's done it for within seconds, and feeling enough confidence to reach out.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, technical, restrained — a "dashboard-technical" synthesis: the precision and mono/live-data feel of an engineering tool, applied to a personal portfolio. Confidence expressed through typographic hierarchy and constraint (one font, one accent color) rather than decoration. Warm exactly once (the glossy 3D hand, the footer's sticker CTA) — everywhere else, quiet and exact.

## Anti-references

- benorth.studio's full sticker/collage aesthetic — scattered stickers, ephemera-as-wallpaper. Explicitly rejected except for one deliberate exception (the footer contact CTA).
- Generic AI-slop defaults: default framework colors, gradient text, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows, numbered section markers (01/02/03) used as reflexive scaffolding, identical card grids, side-stripe borders, glassmorphism as decoration.
- A generic SaaS-cream landing page — the cream body is a deliberate brand choice tied to the dashboard-technical direction and the dark footer contrast, not a default "safe" background.

## Design Principles

- One font, one accent color — restraint comes from constraint, not from confining expressiveness to a single spot.
- Hierarchy is built from scale, weight, and opacity on a single typeface, applied consistently across every section.
- Exactly one deliberate "flashy" object (the 3D hand) and one deliberate sticker exception (the footer CTA) — everything else follows the sharp/mono default.
- Motion is minimal and functional: hover/focus states plus one idle animation, nothing scroll-triggered or ambient.
- Nothing ships that isn't real, verifiable, or maintained by hand (e.g. the mono-readout status line is manually updated, not automated).

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA minimum: ≥4.5:1 text contrast (large text/non-text UI may use the 3:1 large-text allowance where the accent fill is involved), full keyboard navigation, visible focus states, semantic landmarks, alt text on every image, skip-to-main link, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected.
